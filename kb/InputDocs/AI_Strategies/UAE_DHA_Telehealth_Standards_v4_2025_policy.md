## Sec. 17.12.7 (also 4.2.2, 17.3.1(e)) — No narcotic, controlled or semi-controlled medicines via telehealth

The physician shall not prescribe narcotic, controlled or semi-controlled medication through telehealth services.

> "The physician shall not prescribe Narcotic, Controlled or Semi-controlled medication through telehealth services."

*provision id: DHATH-17.12.7 · legal basis: NORME · themes: telehealth, remote prescription, controlled medicine, narcotics · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 17.12.6, 17.13.1(d) — Telehealth prescriptions follow the MOHAP drug list and an online prescription system

Prescribing via telehealth follows the UAE MOHAP drug list, and prescriptions must be issued through an online prescription system with electronic transfer.

*provision id: DHATH-17.12.6 · legal basis: NORME · themes: telehealth, e-prescription · modes: consultation*

## Sec. 10.1, 10.1.1, 10.4.2 — Telehealth consent documented at each encounter

The professional obtains and documents consent from the patient or guardian; electronic consent is acceptable; consent for treatment must be documented at each encounter.

*provision id: DHATH-10 · legal basis: NORME · themes: consent, telehealth, electronic consent, documentation · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 10.2.1, 14.5.5 — Patient identification with at least three identifiers

The patient is identified with at least three identifiers (e.g. full name, date of birth, Emirates ID, record number, phone), and identity is verified and consent granted before diagnosis.

*provision id: DHATH-10.2.1 · legal basis: NORME · themes: patient identification, telehealth · modes: consultation*

## Sec. 11.4, 11.6, 11.10 — Documentation and 25-year retention

All telehealth encounters are documented in the medical record (service type, participants' licence numbers, locations, confirmation of consent) and retained for at least 25 years.

*provision id: DHATH-11 · legal basis: NORME · themes: documentation, retention, audit trail · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 7.6.7, 8.2.5(d), 10.6.4 — Telehealth data stays in the UAE

Telehealth data is stored with a cloud service provider in the UAE; storing, developing or transferring health data outside the country is not permitted unless applicable law allows.

*provision id: DHATH-DATA · legal basis: NORME · themes: data localisation, cloud AI, outside UAE · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 14.1.5(a), 8.3.5, 14.1.6, 9.6.3 — People of determination and understandable language

Provisions must be in place for People of Determination and access must not exclude them; consultations are offered at least in Arabic and English, interpretation services are encouraged, and information is given in a language the patient easily understands.

*provision id: DHATH-14.1.5 · legal basis: NORME · themes: accessible communication, people of determination, deaf, interpretation, pictograms · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 12.4-12.4.3, 14.1.4(c) — Emergency protocols in telehealth

Providers must have tested written protocols for emergency requests, emergency contact numbers, and must not delay advice to seek emergency services.

*provision id: DHATH-12.4 · legal basis: NORME · themes: emergency, crisis, telehealth, urgent referral · modes: crise*

## Sec. 9.8.2 — Explain limits of prescribing without tests

Limitations of prescribing without confirmatory laboratory testing must be explained to the patient.

*provision id: DHATH-9.8.2 · legal basis: NORME · themes: telehealth, patient information · modes: consultation*
