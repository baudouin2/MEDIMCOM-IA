## Boxed warning; 5.4 — Tramadol in pregnancy: neonatal opioid withdrawal syndrome

Prolonged use of tramadol during pregnancy can result in neonatal opioid withdrawal syndrome, which may be life-threatening if not recognised and treated; the prescriber should weigh this risk and inform the patient.

> "Prolonged use of tramadol hydrochloride during pregnancy can result in neonatal opioid withdrawal syndrome"

## Contraindications 4; Warnings 5.5 — Tramadol in children and adolescents

Tramadol is contraindicated in all children younger than 12 years and after tonsillectomy/adenoidectomy in children younger than 18; its use should be avoided in adolescents 12 to 18 years who have other risk factors.

> "Avoid the use of tramadol hydrochloride in adolescents 12 to 18 years of age who have other risk factors"

*provision id: FDA-TRAM-ENFANT · legal basis: RECOMMANDATION · themes: children, adolescents, opioid, tramadol · modes: consultation*

## Dosage and administration 2 — Tramadol maximum daily dose

Tramadol tablets must not be given at a dose exceeding 400 mg per day.

> "Do not administer tramadol hydrochloride tablets at a dose exceeding 400 mg per day."
