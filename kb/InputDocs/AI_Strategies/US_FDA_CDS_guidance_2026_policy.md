## Four non-device criteria — When decision-support software is not a device

Software is non-device clinical decision support when it (1) does not analyse medical images or signals, (2) displays or analyses medical information, (3) supports a health professional's recommendations, and (4) lets the professional independently review the basis of recommendations without relying primarily on them; the 2026 revision places automation-bias concerns under criterion 4.
