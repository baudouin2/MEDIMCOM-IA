## Drug Facts: Warnings — Ibuprofen: stomach bleeding and conditions needing a doctor's advice

Stomach bleeding risk is higher at age 60 or older, with past stomach ulcers or bleeding problems, or when taking a blood thinning (anticoagulant) or steroid drug; ask a doctor before use with high blood pressure, heart disease, liver cirrhosis, kidney disease, asthma or past stroke.

> "take a blood thinning (anticoagulant) or steroid drug"

## Drug Facts: Warnings — Ibuprofen: last 3 months of pregnancy

It is especially important not to use ibuprofen during the last 3 months of pregnancy unless definitely directed by a doctor.
