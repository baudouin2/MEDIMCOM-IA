## Definitions (Supportive Technology) — Supportive technology needs a human final decision

Supportive technology requires a human to make final decisions and be responsible for any actions taken by the technology.

*provision id: DHA21-DEF · legal basis: POLITIQUE · themes: human oversight, final decision, supportive technology · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 4.2.1-4.2.3 — Accountability and appeals

Accountability for outcomes of an AI solution must be agreed; developers are accountable to end users; solutions must have built-in appeal procedures so users can challenge significant decisions.

*provision id: DHA21-4.2 · legal basis: POLITIQUE · themes: accountability, liability, appeal, AI suggestion liability · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 4.3.1 — Failures traced, diagnosed and controlled

Developers must build systems whose failures can be traced, diagnosed and controlled.

*provision id: DHA21-4.3.1 · legal basis: POLITIQUE · themes: traceability, audit trail, logging · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 4.3.2 — Mandatory disclosure, including the professional's role

AI solutions must disclose, among other items, their AI-enabled functions, validation, data sets, algorithm type, the role of the healthcare professional in the final decision and the limitations of data and algorithms.

*provision id: DHA21-4.3.2 · legal basis: POLITIQUE · themes: transparency, disclosure, limitations · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 4.3.3 — Independent third-party validation

All AI solutions for healthcare must undergo independent validation by a competent third party.

*provision id: DHA21-4.3.3 · legal basis: POLITIQUE · themes: validation, certification · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 4.4.1-4.4.2 — Supervised by end users; can be overridden

AI solutions must be safe, secure and supervised by end users, and must be able to be overridden or their decisions reversed by designated end users.

*provision id: DHA21-4.4 · legal basis: POLITIQUE · themes: human oversight, override, validation by doctor · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 4.6.12 — Auditable validation statements

AI tools must include auditable validation statements.

*provision id: DHA21-4.6.12 · legal basis: POLITIQUE · themes: audit, validation · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 4.6.17, 4.8.1-4.8.2, 4.8.5 — Incident reporting and independent audits

Adverse events should be reported in a timely way to oversight bodies; all known or suspected AI incidents or deficiencies, and issues that may affect patient safety, are reported to DHA; regular independent audits are conducted.

*provision id: DHA21-4.8 · legal basis: POLITIQUE · themes: incident reporting, audit, patient safety · modes: crise*
