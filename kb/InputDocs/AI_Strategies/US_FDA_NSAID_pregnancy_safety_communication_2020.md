## Recommendation — Avoid NSAIDs from 20 weeks of pregnancy

Avoid NSAIDs at 20 weeks or later in pregnancy unless specifically advised by a health professional; does not apply to low-dose aspirin; other medicines such as acetaminophen (paracetamol) are available for pain and fever.

> "Other medicines, such as acetaminophen, are available to treat pain and fever during pregnancy."
