## Art. 3 — Professional standard of care

A health practitioner must perform duties with the accuracy and honesty the profession requires, follow recognised scientific and technical standards, give the patient due care and comply with applicable laws.

> "must perform the duties of his job with the level of accuracy and honesty as required by the profession"

*provision id: ML16-A3 · legal basis: LOI · themes: standard of care, duty of care, clinical protocols · modes: consultation*

## Art. 4(1) — Practise within grade and specialisation

The physician must follow the rules, systems and procedures for practising the profession according to grade and specialisation.

*provision id: ML16-A4-1 · legal basis: LOI · themes: licensing, scope of practice, prescriber · modes: consultation*

## Art. 4(2) — Record the patient's condition and history before diagnosis and treatment

Before diagnosis and treatment the physician must record the patient's health condition and personal and family medical history. MEDIMCOM uses this as the legal basis for requiring allergies, current treatments and pregnancy status before a prescription is cleared.

*provision id: ML16-A4-2 · legal basis: LOI · themes: medical history, mandatory information, allergies, pregnancy, current treatments · modes: consultation*

## Art. 4(3) — Use the diagnosis and treatment methods the condition requires

The physician must use the diagnostic and treatment methods required by the patient's condition.

*provision id: ML16-A4-3 · legal basis: LOI · themes: diagnosis, treatment · modes: consultation*

## Art. 4(5) — Inform the patient of treatment options

The physician must keep the patient informed of the available treatment options.

*provision id: ML16-A4-5 · legal basis: LOI · themes: patient information, treatment options, informed consent · modes: consultation*

## Art. 4(6) — Written prescription with dose and usage

The physician must prescribe the medicine, its dose and method of use in writing, with the physician's name, signature and date, and explain the importance of the treatment to the patient or family.

*provision id: ML16-A4-6 · legal basis: LOI · themes: prescription, dose, posology, written prescription, signature, human prescriber · modes: consultation*

## Art. 4(7) — Inform the patient about the nature and seriousness of the condition

The physician must inform the patient of the nature and seriousness of the condition unless this would harm the patient or the patient cannot cope psychologically; family is informed when the patient lacks capacity or cannot be told.

*provision id: ML16-A4-7 · legal basis: LOI · themes: patient information, diagnosis disclosure, accessible communication · modes: consultation*

## Art. 4(8) — Inform about complications before intervention

Before an intervention the physician must inform the patient or family of possible complications, and identify and treat complications where possible.

*provision id: ML16-A4-8 · legal basis: LOI · themes: patient information, complications, informed consent · modes: consultation*

## Art. 5(1) — No treatment without consent, except emergencies

Treating a patient without consent is prohibited, except where emergency intervention is required and consent cannot be obtained, or where the disease is infectious or threatens public health or safety.

> "Treat a patient without his consent except in the cases requiring emergent medical intervention"

*provision id: ML16-A5-1 · legal basis: LOI · themes: consent, informed consent, emergency exception, infectious disease · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 5(5) — No prescription before clinical examination; distance health services

Prescribing any treatment before a clinical examination of the patient is prohibited; the health authorities may set a system for providing distance (remote) health services.

> "Prescribe any treatment before carrying out the clinical examination of the patient."

*provision id: ML16-A5-5 · legal basis: LOI · themes: prescription, clinical examination, telehealth, remote consultation · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 5(10) — No unnecessary procedures without informed consent

Performing unnecessary medical procedures or surgeries without the patient's informed consent is prohibited.

*provision id: ML16-A5-10 · legal basis: LOI · themes: informed consent, procedures · modes: consultation*

## Art. 6 — Definition of medical error

Defines medical error, the basis of a practitioner's liability. It addresses the practitioner's error; it does not allocate responsibility for an AI suggestion validated by the practitioner.

*provision id: ML16-A6 · legal basis: LOI · themes: liability, medical error, AI suggestion liability · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 8(1)(c) — Written consent for surgery

Written consent of the patient is required for surgical interventions.

*provision id: ML16-A8-1c · legal basis: LOI · themes: written consent, surgery, consent form · modes: consultation*

## Art. 16 — No abortion and no prescription that may cause abortion (limited exceptions)

A doctor may neither perform an abortion nor prescribe anything that may cause abortion of a pregnant woman. Exceptions: (1) danger to the mother's life with no other means, decided by an obstetrician with the treating doctor and a written report signed by the spouses; (2) proven serious, incurable fetal deformity, with written consent of both spouses, within 120 days of pregnancy and confirmed by a medical committee.

> "A doctor may neither conduct any abortion operation nor prescribe anything that may cause the abortion"

*provision id: ML16-A16 · legal basis: LOI · themes: pregnancy, abortifacient, prohibited prescription, misoprostol · modes: consultation*

## Art. 23 — Disciplinary sanctions including licence suspension

Provides for disciplinary measures against practitioners, including suspension of the licence.

*provision id: ML16-A23 · legal basis: LOI · themes: sanctions, licence · modes: consultation*

## Art. 25 — Mandatory civil liability insurance

Insurance against civil liability for medical errors is mandatory.

*provision id: ML16-A25 · legal basis: LOI · themes: liability, insurance · modes: consultation, crise*
