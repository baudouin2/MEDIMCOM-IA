## Document level — Cameroon: personal data protection law applicable from 23 June 2026 (comparative)

Cameroon's 2024 personal data law, applicable from 23 June 2026, regulates processing and cross-border transfers; article-level content to be re-read before citation.

## Art. 9 (secondary) — Consent to personal data processing

Consent to processing must be free, informed, specific, unambiguous and express, obtained before processing; parental consent for minors under 18.

*provision id: CM24-A9 · legal basis: LOI · themes: consent, personal data, Cameroon · modes: consultation, crise*

## sensitive data (article to confirm) — Health data are sensitive data

Sensitive data, including health data, may in principle not be processed, except on listed legal bases such as the protection of health.

*provision id: CM24-SENSIBLES · legal basis: LOI · themes: health data, sensitive data, processing, Cameroon · modes: consultation, crise*

## transfers abroad (article to confirm) — Transfers of personal data outside Cameroon

Transfers of personal data to another country require appropriate safeguards and, according to secondary sources, prior authorisation of the data protection authority.

*provision id: CM24-TRANSFERT · legal basis: LOI · themes: cross-border transfer, data localisation, foreign cloud AI, Cameroon · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 53 (secondary) — Personal data protection authority

Creates an independent data protection authority; its organisation is to be set by presidential decree.

*provision id: CM24-A53 · legal basis: LOI · themes: supervisory authority, Cameroon · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 70 (secondary) — Sanctions for breaches

Administrative fines up to 1 billion FCFA for the most serious breaches; criminal sanctions also apply (unauthorised processing, illegal transfers).

*provision id: CM24-A70 · legal basis: LOI · themes: penalties, illegal transfer, Cameroon · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 73 (secondary) — Compliance deadline

Data controllers had 18 months from promulgation (23 December 2024), i.e. until 23 June 2026, to comply.

*provision id: CM24-A73 · legal basis: LOI · themes: entry into force, compliance, Cameroon · modes: consultation, crise*
