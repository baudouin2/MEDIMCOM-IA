## Art. 21(a)-(b),(e) — Accessible information and alternative communication

States provide public information in accessible formats in a timely manner at no extra cost; accept and facilitate sign languages, Braille, augmentative and alternative communication and other accessible means chosen by persons with disabilities in official interactions; recognise and promote sign languages.

> "Accepting and facilitating the use of sign languages, Braille, augmentative and alternative communication,"

## Art. 9 — Accessibility of information and communication

Accessibility applies to the physical environment and to information and communications, including technologies and systems.

*provision id: CRPD-A9 · legal basis: ENGAGEMENT_INTERNATIONAL · themes: accessibility, digital services · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 11 — Situations of risk and humanitarian emergencies

States take all necessary measures to protect persons with disabilities in situations of risk, including armed conflict, humanitarian emergencies and natural disasters.

*provision id: CRPD-A11 · legal basis: ENGAGEMENT_INTERNATIONAL · themes: crisis, emergency, disability, emergency communication · modes: crise*

## Art. 12 — Legal capacity and support in exercising it

Persons with disabilities enjoy legal capacity on an equal basis, and States provide access to the support they may require in exercising it (relevant to consent given through alternative communication).

*provision id: CRPD-A12 · legal basis: ENGAGEMENT_INTERNATIONAL · themes: legal capacity, supported decision-making, consent, pictograms · modes: consultation*

## Art. 25 — Right to health; care on the basis of free and informed consent

Persons with disabilities have the right to the highest attainable standard of health without discrimination; health professionals provide care of the same quality on the basis of free and informed consent.

*provision id: CRPD-A25 · legal basis: ENGAGEMENT_INTERNATIONAL · themes: health services, informed consent, non-discrimination · modes: consultation*
