## Art. 2(1) — Practice of medicine reserved to physicians registered with the Order

No one may practise medicine in Cameroon unless registered on the roll of the Ordre national des médecins.

> "Nul ne peut exercer la profession de médecin au Cameroun s'il n'est pas inscrit au tableau de l'Ordre."

## Art. 4 — Physicians bound by professional secrecy

A physician practising in public administration or the private sector is bound by professional secrecy (scope defined elsewhere).

## Art. 16-17 — Illegal practice of medicine and penalties

Illegal practice includes an unauthorised person habitually taking part in diagnosis or treatment, even in a doctor's presence; penalties: 6 days to 6 months' imprisonment and/or a fine of 200,000 to 2,000,000 FCFA, with immediate cessation.

> "à l'établissement de diagnostics ou aux traitements d'affections par actes professionnels"
