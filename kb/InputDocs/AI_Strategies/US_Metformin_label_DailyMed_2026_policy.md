## 2.1, 2.2 — Metformin maximum daily dose

Adults: up to a maximum of 2550 mg per day in divided doses; paediatric patients (10 years and older): up to 2000 mg per day.

> "up to a maximum dose of 2550 mg per day, given in divided doses."

## 2.3; 4 — Metformin and renal impairment

Metformin is contraindicated in severe renal impairment (eGFR below 30 mL/min/1.73 m2); starting it at an eGFR between 30 and 45 is not recommended; renal function must be assessed before initiation.

> "Severe renal impairment (eGFR below 30 mL/min/1.73 m2)"

## 8.7 — Metformin and hepatic impairment

Metformin is not recommended in patients with hepatic impairment (risk of lactic acidosis).

> "Metformin Hydrochloride Tablets are not recommended in patients with hepatic impairment."

*provision id: DM-METF-FOIE · legal basis: RECOMMANDATION · themes: hepatic impairment, liver, metformin · modes: consultation*
