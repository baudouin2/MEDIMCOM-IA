## Boxed warning — Misoprostol can cause abortion

Misoprostol administered to pregnant women can cause birth defects, abortion, premature birth or uterine rupture; patients must be advised of its abortifacient property.

> "CAN CAUSE BIRTH DEFECTS, ABORTION, PREMATURE BIRTH OR UTERINE RUPTURE."
