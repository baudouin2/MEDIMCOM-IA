## 4 — Amoxicillin and beta-lactam hypersensitivity

Amoxicillin is contraindicated in patients who have had a serious hypersensitivity reaction to amoxicillin or to other beta-lactam antibacterials (penicillins, cephalosporins).

> "contraindicated in patients who have experienced a serious hypersensitivity reaction"

## 2.3 — Amoxicillin and severe renal impairment

No dose reduction unless renal impairment is severe; patients with a glomerular filtration rate below 30 mL/min should not receive the 875 mg dose.

> "Renal impairment patients with a glomerular filtration rate of less than 30 mL/min should NOT receive the 875 mg dose."
