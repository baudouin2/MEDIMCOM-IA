## Sections 1-2 — Abu Dhabi tele-medicine services

The DOH Standard on Tele-Medicine (effective 16 September 2020) covers tele-diagnosis, tele-counseling, tele-medical interventions, tele-consultation, tele-prescription and tele-monitoring, and applies to DOH-licensed or approved facilities.

> "Healthcare facilities licensed or approved by DOH to provide tele-medicine services."
