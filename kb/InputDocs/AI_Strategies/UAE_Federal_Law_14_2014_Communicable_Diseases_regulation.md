## Art. 4 — Duty to notify communicable diseases

Physicians, pharmacists and other practitioners must notify their supervising body no later than 24 hours after knowing or suspecting a Group (A) disease listed in Schedule 1; other persons (contacts, supervisors, facility managers) must notify immediately.

## Art. 5 — Notification by health bodies and facilities

The Ministry, health bodies and private health facilities must notify immediately and no later than 24 hours for Group (A) diseases and 7 days for Group (B) diseases.

*provision id: CD14-A5 · legal basis: LOI · themes: notification, health facility, outbreak · modes: crise*

## Art. 10 — Immediate action and cooperation of health facilities

On notification, the Ministry and health body act immediately (isolation where needed, testing, referral); health facilities must fully cooperate by providing diagnosis, treatment and follow-up information.

*provision id: CD14-A10 · legal basis: LOI · themes: outbreak response, isolation, institutional cooperation · modes: crise*

## Art. 18 — Powers during an epidemic

During an epidemic the Ministry and health body, with the concerned bodies, may declare an infected area, regulate entry and exit, restrict gatherings, regulate public places and apply measures to patients, suspects and contacts.

*provision id: CD14-A18 · legal basis: LOI · themes: emergency powers, epidemic, crisis governance · modes: crise*

## Art. 29 — Confidentiality of infected persons' information

Infected persons are entitled to confidentiality of their disease-related information, which may be disclosed only in cases prescribed by law.

*provision id: CD14-A29 · legal basis: LOI · themes: confidentiality, outbreak, data sharing in crisis · modes: crise*
