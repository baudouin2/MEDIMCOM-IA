## list of exceptions — Exceptions allowing health data outside the UAE

Health data may be stored or transferred abroad in listed cases, including: treatment of the patient abroad; samples sent to foreign laboratories; scientific research under UAE rules; UAE insurers needing data abroad with patient consent; requests by cooperating competent authorities; simple personal medical devices; pharmacovigilance; and other cases approved by the health authorities.

*provision id: RES51-EXC · legal basis: LOI · themes: data localisation exceptions, foreign laboratory, research, telemedicine, cross-border transfer · modes: consultation, crise*

## conditions — Conditions on permitted transfers

Permitted transfers remain subject to controls reported as: written patient consent, limits on sharing, encryption to best standards, and a copy of the data stored inside the country.

*provision id: RES51-COND · legal basis: LOI · themes: data localisation exceptions, encryption, copy in UAE, written consent · modes: consultation, crise*
