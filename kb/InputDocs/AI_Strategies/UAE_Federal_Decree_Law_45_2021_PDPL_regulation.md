## Art. 2(2)(a),(e) — Scope exclusions: government data and regulated health data

The decree-law does not apply to government data or to personal health data that has its own legislation regulating its protection and processing. Health data in MEDIMCOM is therefore governed first by Federal Law No. 2 of 2019.

## Art. 18 — Right to object to automated decisions

A data subject may object to decisions based on automated processing, including profiling, that have legal or seriously adverse effects, unless the processing is agreed in a contract, required by law, or consented to in advance.

*provision id: PDPL-A18 · legal basis: LOI · themes: automated decision, human oversight, profiling, right to object · modes: consultation*

## Arts. 22-23 — Cross-border transfer of (non-health) personal data

Transfers are allowed to countries with adequate protection or under agreements (Art. 22); otherwise under contractual safeguards or explicit consent where not contrary to public or security interests (Art. 23).
