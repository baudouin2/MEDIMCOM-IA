## Factors — AI readiness factors (Report 2.0)

Six readiness factors: data (availability and quality of datasets), research (collaboration between domain and AI research), deployment support (physical, communication and backend infrastructure), standards (trust, interoperability, compliance), open source and code, sandbox environments for experimentation and validation.

*provision id: AIR2-FACTEURS · legal basis: EXEMPLE_MONDIAL · themes: AI readiness, ITU, sandbox, open source · modes: consultation, crise*

## Dimension 6 — Human interface

Ease of human interaction with AI systems; metrics include AI-supported interfaces and interaction channels, local-language model availability, usability, cultural sensitivity and safety safeguards.

*provision id: AIR2-D6 · legal basis: EXEMPLE_MONDIAL · themes: AI readiness, accessibility, human interface, local language · modes: consultation, crise*

## Dimension 8 — Collaboration with AI

Human-AI co-creation and shared decision-making; metrics include feedback loops, trust metrics and comparison of co-created output with human-only or AI-only baselines.

*provision id: AIR2-D8 · legal basis: EXEMPLE_MONDIAL · themes: AI readiness, human oversight, shared decision · modes: consultation, crise*

## Dimension 10 — AI and policies

Testing policies in sandboxes and ensuring policies enable AI integration; metrics include vertical sandboxes and domain-specific policy evaluation, simulation of policy impacts, horizontal policies on data, AI lifecycle and explainability, policy briefs, audits and data sovereignty.

*provision id: AIR2-D10 · legal basis: EXEMPLE_MONDIAL · themes: AI readiness, policy, sandbox, data sovereignty, audit · modes: consultation, crise*

## Dimension 11 — AI for inclusion

Using AI to bridge social divides, including accessibility; metrics include edge AI with privacy and low latency, generative AI support for sign languages and regional languages, offline kits and number of beneficiaries.

*provision id: AIR2-D11 · legal basis: EXEMPLE_MONDIAL · themes: AI readiness, inclusion, accessibility, sign language, deaf · modes: consultation, crise*
