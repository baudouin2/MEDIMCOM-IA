## Art. 7 — Right to communicate and to information through sign language and other means

People of determination have the right to express themselves and to seek, receive and impart information on an equal footing, using Braille, sign language and other means of communication.

*provision id: POD06-A7 · legal basis: LOI · themes: accessible communication, sign language, pictograms, alternative communication, deaf · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 10 — Health and rehabilitation services

People of determination are entitled to health and rehabilitation services, at the State's expense, as set out by the law.

*provision id: POD06-A10 · legal basis: LOI · themes: health services, inclusion · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 13 — Substitute means of communication

The law provides for substitute (alternative) means of communication for people of determination.

*provision id: POD06-A13 · legal basis: LOI · themes: alternative communication, pictograms, deaf · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 20(2) — Simplified materials

Provides for simplified materials adapted to people of determination.

*provision id: POD06-A20 · legal basis: LOI · themes: easy read, simplified information, low literacy · modes: consultation, crise*
