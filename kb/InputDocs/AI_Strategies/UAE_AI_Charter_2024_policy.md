## Principle 6 — Human oversight

The Charter affirms the irreplaceable value of human judgment and human oversight over AI.

*provision id: CHARTER-P6 · legal basis: STRATEGIE · themes: human oversight, national principles · modes: consultation, crise*

## Principles 4-5 — Data privacy and transparency

Privacy of community members is a top priority, and AI systems should be understandable in how they operate and decide.

*provision id: CHARTER-P4-5 · legal basis: STRATEGIE · themes: privacy, transparency · modes: consultation, crise*

## Principles 11-12 — Inclusive access and compliance with treaties and laws

AI awareness for an inclusive future (equitable access for all segments of society) and commitment to international treaties and local laws.

*provision id: CHARTER-P11-12 · legal basis: STRATEGIE · themes: inclusion, accessibility, treaties · modes: consultation, crise*
