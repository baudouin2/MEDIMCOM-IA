## Sec. 3.1.7.2.2 — Clinicians keep ultimate decision-making authority

Clinicians should retain ultimate decision-making authority in all AI-assisted healthcare scenarios.

> "Clinicians should retain ultimate decision-making authority in all AI-assisted healthcare scenarios."

*provision id: RAI25-3.1.7.2.2 · legal basis: NORME · themes: human oversight, final decision, clinician authority, AI suggestion · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 3.1.6.1.1 — Inform patients when AI contributes to decisions

Systems must include mechanisms to inform patients when AI contributes to clinical or operational decisions, including its function, limitations and intended outcomes.

*provision id: RAI25-3.1.6.1.1 · legal basis: NORME · themes: transparency, patient information, AI disclosure · modes: consultation*

## Sec. 3.1.6.1.3 — Consent mechanisms and opt-out for AI-driven services

Clear consent mechanisms must ensure informed decision-making and transparency; patients are told they can opt out of AI-driven services where applicable.

*provision id: RAI25-3.1.6.1.3 · legal basis: NORME · themes: consent, opt-out, AI disclosure · modes: consultation*

## Sec. 3.1.7.1.3 — All decisions logged and traceable

All decisions, changes, updates and interventions must be logged and traceable to a responsible party.

> "All decisions, changes, updates, and interventions must be logged and traceable to a responsible party."

*provision id: RAI25-3.1.7.1.3 · legal basis: NORME · themes: logging, audit trail, traceability, accountability · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 3.1.7.1.2.3 — Report adverse outcomes and ethical concerns

Governance includes reporting adverse outcomes or ethical concerns to governance bodies.

*provision id: RAI25-3.1.7.1.2.3 · legal basis: NORME · themes: incident reporting, adverse event · modes: crise*

## Sec. 3.1.4.5.1 — Compute and storage in the UAE

Compute and storage infrastructure, including disaster-recovery sites, must be located in the UAE; remote access for development and support teams should be exclusively from within the UAE.

*provision id: RAI25-3.1.4.5.1 · legal basis: NORME · themes: data localisation, cloud AI, hosting, outside UAE · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 3.1.5.1.1 — Datasets reflect population diversity

All training, validation and test datasets must reflect the diversity of the target population.

*provision id: RAI25-3.1.5.1.1 · legal basis: NORME · themes: bias, inclusion, datasets, deaf users · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 3.1.3.1.1 — Validation before deployment

AI models shall undergo rigorous validation against established performance benchmarks before deployment.

*provision id: RAI25-3.1.3.1.1 · legal basis: NORME · themes: validation, testing, deployment · modes: consultation, crise*

## Sec. 3.3.1.2.2 — Risk classes: very high risk prohibited, high risk assessed

AI systems with very high residual risk must be prohibited from deployment; high-risk systems require conformity assessments before and after deployment.

*provision id: RAI25-3.3.1.2.2 · legal basis: NORME · themes: risk classification, conformity assessment · modes: consultation, crise*
