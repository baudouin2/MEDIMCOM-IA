## Art. 12 — Automatic logging over the lifetime

High-risk AI systems shall technically allow for the automatic recording of events (logs) over their lifetime, to detect risks and substantial modifications and support post-market monitoring.

> "High-risk AI systems shall technically allow for the automatic recording of events (logs) over the lifetime of the system."

## Art. 14 — Human oversight, including awareness of automation bias

High-risk AI systems are designed so that natural persons can effectively oversee them, prevent or minimise risks to health, safety and fundamental rights, remain aware of automation bias, and decide not to use or to override the output.

*provision id: AIA-A14 · legal basis: EXEMPLE_MONDIAL · themes: human oversight, automation bias, override · modes: consultation, crise*

## Arts. 57-59 — AI regulatory sandboxes and use of personal data in them

Member States set up AI regulatory sandboxes; personal data lawfully collected for other purposes may be used in a sandbox only for substantial public interest (including public health), when anonymised or synthetic data cannot meet the need, in an isolated environment, with logs, no effects on data subjects and deletion at the end.

*provision id: AIA-A57-59 · legal basis: EXEMPLE_MONDIAL · themes: regulatory sandbox, synthetic data, data localisation, innovation · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 73 — Reporting serious incidents

Providers of high-risk AI systems report serious incidents to market surveillance authorities immediately once a causal link is established or reasonably likely, and no later than 15 days after becoming aware (shorter limits for widespread or fatal incidents).

*provision id: AIA-A73 · legal basis: EXEMPLE_MONDIAL · themes: incident reporting, AI incident, crisis · modes: crise*
