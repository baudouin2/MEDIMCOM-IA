## Art. 1 (definition of Medical Equipment) — AI-based products can be medical equipment

Medical Equipment is a medical product containing a substance, device, instrument, implant or system, including accessories and operating software, and includes wearable devices and products based on AI technology.

> "shall include wearable devices and products based on AI technology"

*provision id: MP24-A1-EQ · legal basis: LOI · themes: software as medical device, AI medical device, regulatory status, SaMD · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 1 (definition of Prescription) — A prescription is issued by a licensed practitioner

A prescription is a document issued by a licensed practitioner to a practitioner authorised to dispense; verbal orders must later be documented as set by ministerial resolution.

*provision id: MP24-A1-RX · legal basis: LOI · themes: prescription, licensed prescriber, human prescriber · modes: consultation*

## Art. 5(1) — Marketing approval required

Importing, distributing, selling, using or manufacturing medical products for circulation is prohibited without marketing approval from the Emirates Drug Establishment.

*provision id: MP24-A5-1 · legal basis: LOI · themes: marketing authorisation, unregistered medicine, medicine approval · modes: consultation*

## Arts. 11-13 — Emergency use approval

The decree-law provides for emergency use approval of medical products.

*provision id: MP24-A11-13 · legal basis: LOI · themes: emergency use, crisis, medicine approval · modes: crise*

## Art. 42(2) — Prescribing only when authorised

Licensed health practitioners may not advise on, prescribe or dispense medical products unless authorised under the decree-law and its regulations.

*provision id: MP24-A42-2 · legal basis: LOI · themes: prescriber, licensing, prescription · modes: consultation*

## Art. 55 — Controlled products only on a licensed human physician's prescription

Pharmacists may dispense controlled products for patients only on a prescription from a licensed human physician (separate rules for veterinary use).

*provision id: MP24-A55 · legal basis: LOI · themes: controlled medicine, human prescriber, narcotics · modes: consultation, crise*
