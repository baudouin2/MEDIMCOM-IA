## Drug Facts: Warnings — Acetaminophen: liver warning and maximum daily dose

Severe liver damage may occur above 4,000 mg of acetaminophen in 24 hours, with other acetaminophen-containing drugs, or with 3 or more alcoholic drinks a day; ask a doctor before use in liver disease and a doctor or pharmacist when taking warfarin.
