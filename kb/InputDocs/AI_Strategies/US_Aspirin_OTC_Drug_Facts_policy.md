## Drug Facts: Warnings — Aspirin: Reye's syndrome in children and teenagers

Children and teenagers who have or are recovering from chicken pox or flu-like symptoms should not use aspirin (Reye's syndrome, rare but serious).

> "Children and teenagers who have or are recovering from chicken pox or flu-like symptoms should not use this product."

## Drug Facts: Warnings — Aspirin: last 3 months of pregnancy

It is especially important not to use aspirin during the last 3 months of pregnancy unless definitely directed by a doctor.

> "It is especially important not to use aspirin during the last 3 months of pregnancy."

*provision id: OTC-ASA-GROSS · legal basis: RECOMMANDATION · themes: aspirin, pregnancy · modes: consultation*

## Drug Facts: Warnings — Aspirin: stomach bleeding and maximum daily dose

Stomach bleeding risk is higher with past stomach ulcers or bleeding problems or when taking a blood thinning (anticoagulant) or steroid drug; adults: no more than 12 tablets of 325 mg in 24 hours unless directed by a doctor.

> "do not take more than 12 tablets in 24 hours unless directed by a doctor"
