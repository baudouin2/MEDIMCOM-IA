## Policy statement (AI as supportive technology) — AI supports; a human decides

AI in healthcare is treated as supportive technology: it requires a human to make final decisions and be responsible for any actions taken by the technology.

> "it requires a human to make final decisions and be responsible for any actions taken by the technology"

## Policy statement (verifiable and explainable) — Verifiable and explainable AI; developers accountable

AI software should be verifiable and explainable; developers are accountable; major versions require certification and re-certification; validation must be auditable; regular audits and feedback to DoH.

*provision id: DOH18-EXPL · legal basis: POLITIQUE · themes: explainability, accountability, validation, audit · modes: consultation, crise*

## Policy statement (graceful degradation) — Graceful degradation

AI solutions should degrade gracefully when components fail, so that care continues safely.

*provision id: DOH18-DEGRADE · legal basis: POLITIQUE · themes: graceful degradation, outage, resilience, crisis · modes: crise*
