## Art. 3 — Deaf persons are persons with a sensory disability

Sensory disabilities include deaf, deaf-mute, mute and hard-of-hearing persons.

> "sourds, sourds - muets, muets, malentendants"

## Art. 22 — State coverage of health care for eligible persons with disabilities

Indigent persons holding a national disability card receive total or partial State coverage of rehabilitation, consultations, laboratory tests, imaging, hospitalisation, evacuations and some medicines.

> "une prise en charge totale ou partielle par l'état"

*provision id: CM10-A22 · legal basis: LOI · themes: health care access, disability, Cameroon · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 32 — Access of persons with disabilities to information technologies

The State, decentralised authorities and civil society must facilitate access of persons with disabilities to information technologies.

> "L'accès des personnes handicapées aux technologies de l'information"
