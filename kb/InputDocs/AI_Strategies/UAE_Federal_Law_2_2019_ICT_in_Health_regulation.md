## Art. 4 — Confidentiality, integrity and availability of health data

When using ICT in health: (1) keep health data confidential and do not circulate it outside permitted cases; (2) ensure its validity and credibility by protecting it from unauthorised damage, amendment, alteration, deletion or addition; (3) ensure availability to authorised persons.

> "To ensure the validity and credibility of the health data and information"

*provision id: ICT19-A4 · legal basis: LOI · themes: confidentiality, integrity, tamper-evident log, audit trail, availability · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 13 — Health data may not be stored or processed outside the State

Health data relating to health services provided in the UAE may not be stored, processed, generated or transferred outside the State, except where a resolution is issued by the Health Authority in coordination with the Ministry. (MEDIMCOM interpretation, not the text: sending consultation content to a cloud AI service hosted abroad is processing outside the State.)

> "It is not permissible to store, process, generate or transform the health data and information outside State"

*provision id: ICT19-A13 · legal basis: LOI · themes: data localisation, cross-border transfer, cloud AI, foreign server, outside UAE · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 16 — Use of patient information only for health purposes without written approval

Patient information must be kept confidential and used only for health purposes without the patient's written approval, except: (1) insurers or funders reviewing entitlements; (2) scientific and clinical research without disclosing identity; (3) preventive and curative public-health measures or protecting the patient or contacts; (4) request of judicial authorities; (5) Health Authority request for monitoring, inspection and public health.

*provision id: ICT19-A16 · legal basis: LOI · themes: confidentiality, secondary use, commercial use, written consent, research, public health, insurance, judicial request · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 20(1) — Retention of health data for at least 25 years

Health data kept through ICT must be retained for a period commensurate with need and not less than 25 years from the last health procedure, with confidentiality, validity and credibility ensured.

*provision id: ICT19-A20 · legal basis: LOI · themes: retention, audit log retention, records · modes: consultation, crise*

## Art. 24 — Penalty for violating the localisation rule

Violating Article 13 is punishable by a fine of AED 500,000 to AED 700,000.

*provision id: ICT19-A24 · legal basis: LOI · themes: penalty, data localisation, fine · modes: consultation, crise*
