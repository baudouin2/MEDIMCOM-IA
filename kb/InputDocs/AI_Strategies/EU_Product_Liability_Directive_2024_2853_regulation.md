## Arts. 4, 9, 10 — Software is a product; disclosure and presumption of defect

Software, including AI and software-as-a-service, is a product for strict liability; courts can order disclosure of evidence (Art. 9) and presume defectiveness when evidence is not disclosed or safety requirements are breached (Art. 10).

> "includes electricity, digital manufacturing files, raw materials and software"
