## Art. 6(1) — Notify WHO within 24 hours

Each State Party notifies WHO, through its National IHR Focal Point, within 24 hours of assessment of events that may constitute a public health emergency of international concern.

## Art. 45(1) — Confidentiality of personal health information under the IHR

Health information about identifiable persons collected or received under the IHR is kept confidential and processed anonymously as required by national law.

> "which refers to an identified or identifiable person shall be kept confidential"
