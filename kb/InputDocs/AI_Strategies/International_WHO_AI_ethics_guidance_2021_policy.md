## Six guiding principles — WHO principles for AI in health

Protect human autonomy; promote human well-being, safety and the public interest; ensure transparency, explainability and intelligibility; foster responsibility and accountability; ensure inclusiveness and equity; promote AI that is responsive and sustainable.
